Dear Nick,

My dearest Nick,

I have been wanting to tell you so many things, and I thought that creating this little website would be a special way to put some of them into words.

First, thank you. Thank you for being part of my life and for giving me so many reasons to smile. Thank you for the conversations, the laughter, the little moments, and even the ordinary days that somehow become special simply because I get to share them with you.

I hope you know that I notice the little things, the things you do without expecting anything in return. The way you make an effort, the way you listen, and the way you make time for me despite us being literally 12 hours apart. I notice all the little gestures that might seem ordinary to you but mean so much to me.

I appreciate you more than I probably express. I appreciate your presence (even though it's over the phone lang), your patience, your kindness, and the person that you are. I don't need everything to be perfect. I just want us to keep learning, growing, understanding each other, and making more memories.

I hope I can be someone who makes you feel as appreciated as you make me feel. I hope you never have to question whether you matter to me, because you do. Even on busy days, difficult days, and days when words are not enough, I hope you remember that you are someone I am genuinely grateful for.

If I could ask for one thing, it would be to keep collecting little moments with you. More random conversations, more laughter, more adventures, more food trips, more pictures, and more memories that we can look back on someday.

I made this because I wanted you to have something you could revisit whenever you need a reminder of how loved and appreciated you are.

You are important to me, Nick. Thank you for being you, and thank you for being part of my life.

With all my love,
Isabelle ♡
